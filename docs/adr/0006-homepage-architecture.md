# ADR-06: Homepage at N=1, featured viewport then slate then newsletter then footer

Homepage order: (1) the Featured Work, full bleed, first viewport, carrying exactly four text elements: the hero promise as a quiet small line, the title, the fused status-year line, and one functional verb ("Notify Me" anchoring the newsletter); (2) the remaining slate under its status header ("In Development" with the object tile); (3) the newsletter as a first-class object; (4) the plain sitemap footer with the descriptor line. No label statement, no "what we make" explainer, no manifesto: outside the hero promise, the homepage contains zero sentences about Vixio.

## Considered options

- Hero promise omitted from the homepage entirely (the strictest A24 reading): rejected narrowly; the Compass assigns "Worlds worth entering." to the public hero surface, and one quiet line inside the featured viewport satisfies both canons. It is a promise about the work, not a description of the label.
- A contact CTA section at the page bottom (the previous design): rejected; contact is chrome (nav and footer), not catalog. The newsletter is the homepage's relationship channel.

## Consequences

The page h1 is the Featured Work's title: the slate IS the homepage, including semantically. At one featured film plus one section this is honest at N=1; at N=50 the same architecture renders more status sections from data with no structural change. Momentum framing throughout: every visible state describes work in motion, never absence.
