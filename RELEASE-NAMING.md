# Release Naming: the first film

Ten title candidates for the first Vixio release, currently shipping publicly as "Untitled Vixio Film, 2026" (ADR-04). Constraints applied to every candidate: no banned words, no real IP or franchise names, no internal-codename echoes, and nothing that names the source world of the piece (the tribute subject stays unnamed in public until the lawyer read clears it). Each candidate shows how it reads on a slate tile and in a festival listing.

| # | Title | Rationale | On a tile | In a festival listing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Before the Cut** | The founder's own working concept: the held moment before a blade falls and before an editor's cut. Doubly cinematic, owns both meanings. Provenance note: this phrase appears in internal planning docs as the concept title; it is a working title, not a codename, but the founder should confirm comfort with that lineage. | Before the Cut, 2026 | Before the Cut (Hong Kong, 2026, short) |
| 2 | **Whetstone** | The stone that sharpens the blade: preparation, discipline, craft before the act. One word, hard consonants, sits beautifully in Space Grotesk. | Whetstone, 2026 | Whetstone (Hong Kong, 2026, short) |
| 3 | **Threshold** | A doorway about to be crossed; quietly carries the label promise of worlds worth entering without quoting it. | Threshold, 2026 | Threshold (Hong Kong, 2026, short) |
| 4 | **The First Room** | A world begins with one room; honest about being a first release while pointing at the world, not the label. | The First Room, 2026 | The First Room (Hong Kong, 2026, short) |
| 5 | **The Drawn Line** | A blade drawn, a line of ink drawn: the film's craft thesis in four words. | The Drawn Line, 2026 | The Drawn Line (Hong Kong, 2026, short) |
| 6 | **Night Practice** | Solitary discipline after dark; modest, concrete, atmospheric. The kind of title a quiet masterpiece carries. | Night Practice, 2026 | Night Practice (Hong Kong, 2026, short) |
| 7 | **Held Breath** | The instant of stillness before motion, which is the film's emotional engine. Two syllables of suspense. | Held Breath, 2026 | Held Breath (Hong Kong, 2026, short) |
| 8 | **Doorway of Light** | Echoes the planned key image (a doorway of light in white haze); title and key art would land as one move. Softer and more lyrical than the others. | Doorway of Light, 2026 | Doorway of Light (Hong Kong, 2026, short) |
| 9 | **The Empty Seat** | One cinema seat waiting for a viewer: the audience as the missing piece of the world. Slightly meta; strongest if the seat image ships as key art. | The Empty Seat, 2026 | The Empty Seat (Hong Kong, 2026, short) |
| 10 | **Stillwater** | Surface calm over depth; one word, easy to say in any market, reads well beside a Hong Kong production credit. Risk: faint echo of other works named Stillwater (no franchise conflict, but search results are shared). | Stillwater, 2026 | Stillwater (Hong Kong, 2026, short) |

## Recommendation

**Before the Cut.** It is the only candidate that already belongs to the film (the founder conceived it), it carries the double meaning a film-craft label should want, and it reads cleanly at every size from tile to festival catalog. If the founder prefers a title with no internal lineage at all, **Whetstone** is the strongest fresh pick: hardest consonants, clearest craft thesis, best single-word tile presence.

Swapping the title is a one-field change in `lib/slate.ts` (ADR-05).
